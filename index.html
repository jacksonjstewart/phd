The Stacks

Hinc lucem et pocula sacra
University of Cambridge · est. 1209 · From this place, we gain enlightenment and precious knowledge

This record holds you to the standard of the world's finest doctoral programmes in clinical medicine and biomedical science. The expectations documented here are drawn from Harvard BBS, Yale MD-PhD, Oxford DPhil, and Cambridge PhD requirements — not as a comparison, but as a floor. The work speaks for itself.

I
The Canon
Harvard BBS requires BBS 230 — a full course in biological literature analysis and experimental design. Oxford DPhil mandates departmental journal club attendance throughout candidacy. Self-imposed standard: 50 landmark ID and antifungal trials read, annotated, and formally critiqued.
— of 50
Programme Expectations
Harvard BBS BBS 230 — full course in biological literature analysis and experimental design. 12 seminal papers per rotation, 3 rotations minimum.
Oxford DPhil Mandatory departmental journal club attendance throughout candidacy. No specified paper count — sustained engagement expected.
Yale MD-PhD Journal clubs embedded across pre-clinical years. Critical appraisal of primary literature is a core competency evaluated at qualifying exam.
Cambridge PhD Weekly departmental seminars and paper presentations required. Transfer of Status assessed on depth of literature engagement.
Your Standard 50 landmark ID and antifungal trials — read, annotated, and formally critiqued. Written critique required per paper, not passive reading.
II
The Credential
Yale MD-PhD requires submission of a first-author manuscript before graduation — 184 of 196 graduates between 2010 and 2024 achieved this. Board certification in Infectious Diseases Pharmacotherapy is held by fewer than 2,000 pharmacists worldwide. Examination target: 2026.

Programme Expectations
Yale MD-PhD First-author manuscript required before resuming clinical training. 184 of 196 graduates achieved this between 2010–2024.
Harvard BBS One semester mandatory teaching fellowship. Research proposal development course (BBS 330qc) required before qualifying exam.
Oxford DPhil Transfer of Status by term 4 requires demonstrated command of literature and preliminary results. Confirmation at term 9 includes publications review.
Cambridge PhD First-year registration requires evidence of scholarly progress. Board certification equivalent: DPhil/PhD degree plus clinical fellowship training.
Your Standard BCIDP board certification — 39 modules across Group 1 and 2. Fewer than 2,000 pharmacists certified globally. Examination target: 2026.
III
The Clinic
Oxford DPhil requires original research of a kind expected of a capable and diligent student after three to four years of full-time study. Cambridge PhD students produce reference tools with real-world clinical application. Self-imposed standard: five completed clinical reference tools deployed or published.
— complete
Programme Expectations
Oxford DPhil Original research of a kind expected of a capable and diligent student after 3–4 years of full-time study. Clinical translation explicitly valued.
Cambridge PhD Thesis-by-publication model permitted. Students expected to produce reference tools and resources with real-world clinical application.
Harvard BBS Translational output valued. Rotations in clinical labs expose students to bedside-to-bench knowledge translation as a research competency.
Yale MD-PhD MD-PhD graduates expected to bridge clinical and laboratory science. Clinical tool development counts toward scholarly output portfolio.
Your Standard Five completed clinical reference tools deployed or published — Special Routes, TxPx, HPTP, Learn ID modules, AHD materials. Built from clinical need, not academic exercise.
IV
The Record
Harvard BBS mandates one full semester of teaching fellowship. Yale MD-PhD requires a first-author manuscript submitted before resuming clinical training. Self-imposed standard: three manuscripts submitted, five FoPPS lectures delivered, two professional society memberships active by thesis defence.
— complete
Programme Expectations
Harvard BBS One full semester mandatory teaching fellowship. Pedagogy training required prior. Teaching viewed as a core scholarly competency, not optional.
Yale MD-PhD First-author manuscript submitted before graduation. Grant writing workshop series required before year 4. Annual NIH F30/F31 application encouraged.
Oxford DPhil Confirmation of Status at term 9 requires evidence of publications and conference presentations. Scholarly output assessed by two independent examiners.
Cambridge PhD Thesis 40,000–80,000 words. Viva voce with internal and external examiner. Published papers may be incorporated directly into thesis as connected argument.
Your Standard Three manuscripts submitted, five FoPPS lectures delivered, two professional society memberships active, one grant application per PhD year — by thesis defence.
V
The Field
Harvard BBS students rotate through three independent laboratories in year one — building research breadth before committing to depth. Oxford DPhil Confirmation requires evidence of conference presentations and active scholarly contribution. Self-imposed standard: three concurrent active research fronts maintained throughout the PhD.
— active fronts
Programme Expectations
Harvard BBS Three independent lab rotations in year 1, minimum 8 weeks each. Breadth before depth — thesis advisor not selected until rotations complete.
Oxford DPhil Confirmation of Status at term 9 requires conference presentations and active scholarly contribution beyond the primary thesis project.
Yale MD-PhD Research committee meets twice annually. Students expected to maintain awareness of parallel literature and adjacent research fronts to their thesis.
Cambridge PhD Departmental seminars and wider academic community engagement required. Intellectual breadth expected beyond the narrow thesis question.
Your Standard Three concurrent active research fronts maintained throughout the PhD — Allergy/Delabel, ASP/Stewardship, and primary PhD program — with wastewater and scholarly writing as parallel threads.
